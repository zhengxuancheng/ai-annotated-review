# Code Of Conduct

This project uses a practical contributor standard: be clear, respectful, and evidence-based.

Expected behavior:

- discuss technical disagreements with concrete evidence,
- keep criticism focused on code, docs, tests, product claims, or release risk,
- respect privacy boundaries and do not post private documents or secrets,
- avoid harassment, insults, discriminatory language, and personal attacks.

Unacceptable behavior:

- publishing another person's private information,
- posting credentials, tokens, or private documents,
- sustained off-topic disruption,
- abusive or discriminatory comments,
- knowingly making false claims about platform capabilities, security, approval status, or user data handling.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may limit participation when behavior harms the project or contributors.
